Top story · Frontier models

Anthropic and OpenAI both cut into the cost of frontier capability

Reported fact: Anthropic launched Claude Opus 5.5 on September 22. Anthropic's page identifies it as the first Claude 5.5 model and says it matches Claude Fable 5.1 on most work while costing 40% less to run than Opus 5. OpenRouter's machine-readable catalog records the new model object at 16:32:12 UTC on September 22. OpenAI also launched GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna on September 22; OpenRouter records their model objects at 18:12:55 UTC and 18:13:06 UTC respectively. OpenAI says both are 50% cheaper than their GPT-5.6 promotional API prices.

What changed: two frontier labs simultaneously pushed a familiar strategy further: make high-end reasoning and coding capability cheaper enough to support much longer agent workloads. Anthropic also made Opus 5.5 available through major clouds and GitHub Copilot; OpenAI put Sol/Luna into ChatGPT Work, Codex and its API.

Analysis: The important competitive variable is increasingly cost per successful task, not benchmark score alone. When two major labs release cheaper frontier tiers in the same 24-hour window, the economic pressure shifts from “who has the smartest model?” toward “which model can sustain the most useful work per dollar?” That directly affects agent builders, inference providers and SaaS margins.
Anthropic: Claude Opus 5.5 · OpenAI: GPT-6 Sol and Luna · Machine-readable: OpenRouter · Independent: Reuters on Opus 5.5

Security / cybercrime

Microsoft disrupts EvilTokens, an AI-assisted phishing and fraud platform

Reported fact: Microsoft published its disruption report on September 22. Microsoft says EvilTokens had been linked to more than 12,000 compromised inboxes across more than 10,000 organizations and used an AI chatbot to analyze stolen mailboxes, identify trusted relationships and recommend fraud strategies. Microsoft says it seized 50 operating websites and disabled more than 150 supporting domains with partners; UK police arrested two men in connection with the alleged operation.

Status: The operational disruption and Microsoft's observations are confirmed by Microsoft. The criminal activity and attribution are allegations described by Microsoft and law enforcement, not a judicial finding established here.
Analysis: The significance is the packaging. AI is being used not only to generate phishing text but to compress reconnaissance, target selection and fraud preparation into a service. That makes agent security and identity controls increasingly inseparable from conventional cybersecurity.

Amazon's action itself predates the reporting window, beginning September 20, so the event is included as a consequential development newly surfaced and independently verified during this window rather than a claim that the block began today.

Freshness note: This is deliberately labeled as a verification exception.
